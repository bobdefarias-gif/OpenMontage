# Entregável 6: guia para pais e educadores

O texto abaixo vai **para o livro** (edição EN), nas p.4–5 e nas caixas ao longo das atividades. Não faz alegações médicas nem promete resultados de desenvolvimento.

## p.4 — Hello, grown-ups!
*Leon's Thinking Book* is a playful activity book for children aged 4 to 6. Its idea is simple: **learning to think, not just learning to answer.**

Inside, Leon and Luz invite your child to nine islands of games. On each island, your child can practise one way of thinking:

- **Look Closely:** noticing small details.
- **Remember:** keeping pictures and words in mind.
- **Shapes & Colors:** naming and comparing shapes, colors and sizes.
- **Patterns:** seeing what repeats and what comes next.
- **Logic:** sorting, matching and explaining "why".
- **Numbers:** counting, comparing and adding small amounts.
- **Words:** sounds, letters, rhymes and first words.
- **Space:** where things are and how they fit.
- **Feelings:** naming feelings and finding ways to calm down.

Each island opens with a painting by artist Robert de Farias, the author of this book. Take a moment to look at it together. There are no wrong answers when you talk about art.

**Before you start**
- Keep sessions short: 10 to 15 minutes is plenty.
- Read the instructions aloud. Most children of this age don't read yet.
- Children can answer in different ways: by pointing, saying, drawing, coloring or writing. All of them count.
- Pages are marked with stars:
  - ★ I Can Do It!
  - ★★ Let Me Think!
  - ★★★ Big Challenge!
- You don't have to go in order. Skipping a page and coming back later is fine.

## p.5 — How to help without giving the answer
**Ask, don't tell.** Try questions like:
- *"What do you see?"*
- *"How did you find it?"*
- *"Is there another way?"*
- *"What would Leon do?"*

**Give a small clue, not the answer.**
- Point to one row, or cover part of the page.
- Say: *"Look at the top. Is it there?"*

**Mistakes are part of thinking.** Leon makes mistakes too, and then he tries again. When your child gets something wrong, you can say:
- *"Interesting! Let's check together."*
- *"Oops, like Leon! What could we try now?"*

**Praise the effort.** Instead of "Clever!", try:
- *"You looked very carefully!"*
- *"You didn't give up!"*
- *"You found a new way!"*

**Adapt to your child.**
- Younger children (4): start with ★ pages, point instead of writing, and do half a page.
- Older children (5–6): try the ★★★ pages and the "Try Another Way" boxes, and ask them to explain their answers.

**Answers** are at the back of the book (pages 91–95). Some pages have many good answers. Talk about them together!

*This book offers playful practice. It is not a test, a diagnosis or a substitute for advice from a teacher or health professional.*

## Caixas ao longo do livro
**Formato:**
- 1 caixa por página, no máximo.
- Máximo 25 palavras.
- Fundo creme com borda ouro.
- Rótulo em Andika Bold.

| Tipo | Para quem | Quando usar |
|---|---|---|
| **Parent's Little Tip** | adulto | como apoiar sem dar a resposta |
| **Ask Leon** | criança (lida pelo adulto) | uma pergunta extra de raciocínio |
| **Talk About It** | os dois | conversa, sobretudo em Feelings |
| **Try Another Way** | criança | extensão opcional para quem quer mais |

**Frequência:**
- Cerca de 1 em cada 2 páginas tem uma caixa.
- Todas as páginas ★★★ têm um "Parent's Little Tip".
- Todas as páginas de Feelings têm um "Talk About It".
