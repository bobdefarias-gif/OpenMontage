## Description (paste into KDP; the HTML tags are supported by KDP)

```html
<b>Learning to think, not just learning to answer.</b>

<p>Looking for screen-free activities that still feel like play? Leon the lion and his friend Luz invite children aged 4 to 6 on a playful journey through 9 islands of thinking games. All you need is a pencil.</p>

<p><b>Inside this 96-page activity book:</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Look Closely</b> – spot the difference, find the shadow, odd one out</li>
<li><b>Remember</b> – look-and-remember games and listening stories</li>
<li><b>Shapes &amp; Colors</b> – shapes, sizes, color by number, tracing</li>
<li><b>Patterns</b> – what comes next, bead necklaces, make your own pattern</li>
<li><b>Logic</b> – riddles, sorting, first-next-last, picture grids</li>
<li><b>Numbers</b> – counting, adding, number paths, dot to dot</li>
<li><b>Words</b> – letters, first sounds, rhymes, first words</li>
<li><b>Space</b> – positions, mirror drawing, mazes</li>
<li><b>Feelings</b> – naming feelings, calm breathing, kind choices</li>
<li>A <b>Big Challenge</b> island, a star chart and a certificate at the end</li>
</ul>

<p><b>What makes it different</b></p>
<ul>
<li><b>Real art:</b> each island opens with a painting by the artist and author Robert de Farias, with an "Art Detective" question to look at it together.</li>
<li><b>Characters with a purpose:</b> Leon tries, makes mistakes and tries again. Luz asks questions and celebrates new ideas.</li>
<li><b>Three levels:</b> I Can Do It! · Let Me Think! · Big Challenge!, so every child can start where they are.</li>
<li><b>Made for grown-ups too:</b> a short guide, little tips on the pages ("Ask Leon", "Talk About It", "Try Another Way") and answers at the back that you can check in seconds.</li>
<li>Children can answer by pointing, saying, drawing, coloring or writing. No reading is needed for most pages.</li>
</ul>

<p>Perfect for home, travel, waiting rooms and quiet afternoons. Short pages of 10 to 15 minutes, with no screen and no charger.</p>

<p><b>Looking for the next step?</b> Leon's Thinking Book, Volume 2 (ages 6–8) offers 12 missions with codes, sudoku, maps and planning puzzles. Also available in French and Dutch.</p>

<p><i>This book offers playful practice. It is not a test, a diagnosis or a substitute for advice from a teacher or health professional.</i></p>
```
